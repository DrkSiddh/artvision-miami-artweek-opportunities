# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment: Miami Art Week Opportunities

Hey, so I was grabbing a coffee the other day and thinking about how wild Miami Art Week is coming up—November 30th through December 6th, the whole city buzzing with Art Basel and all the satellite fairs. And I realized there are still some really solid, open doors for artists who want to get their work in front of people without breaking the bank.

Let me tell you what I found, friend to friend.

First up, if you're into digital work and the whole decentralized tech side of things, Render Network has this open call called Miami After Dark. They're picking 10 to 15 artists for a massive 50-foot projection on Miami Beach at the National Hotel on December 2nd. Theme is Miami After Dark—think neon, night energy, that after-hours vibe. No fee at all. They even cover the rendering costs with credits on their network, because the pieces have to be rendered there. It's open mainly to US artists in some listings, but check the form. Deadline is November 15th. What they're looking for is original digital pieces up to two minutes long. Go to the Typeform: https://renderfoundation.typeform.com/to/NGgLRjhY or just Google "Render Network Miami After Dark open call". It takes about seven minutes and has 9 questions. You'll need to share your name, email, artist name, a portfolio website or Google Drive folder with examples of previous work, and details about the piece itself—nothing crazy, just the essentials.

Then there's One Love Art DAO with their FRONTERA open call. These folks are a nonprofit 501(c)(3) global art collective with a digital gallery at the YVE Hotel in Miami. They're seeking works that challenge our understanding and shift perspectives. Theme is FRONTERA. Selected artists get exhibited in their gallery at the hotel during Miami Art Week (Dec 1-6), and considered for partner opportunities. Completely free. They welcome global artists, and NFT or unminted works on any chain are fine. Deadline November 15th. Form is here: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdqPVMlUMsAbZOW3v48Z8tacY-nSzYWsSUckDA_2ryFQ17H3g/viewform or the short link forms.gle/DjERA61QPdZEf19SA. Google "One Love Art DAO FRONTERA Miami". Expect to drop a few portfolio links or images, a short bio or statement, and basic contact info—probably around 5-8 questions and 1-3 receipts. Note: the form may ask you to sign in with Google.

If you want something on Artsy during the week, Nova Gallery has an online exhibition running December 3rd to January 5th. There's a $40 application fee, but it's because you get exposure on Artsy (big collector platform), 70% of any sales, press to over 75,000 contacts, and possible non-exclusive representation. Prestigious enough that the fee makes sense for the access. Deadline around November 20-26. Looking for contemporary work in any medium including digital. Apply via ArtConnect: https://www.artconnect.com/opportunity/gOFrOmD67OnaUQ_VOTjrH. Up to 5 images plus the fee.

And over in Downtown Miami, Aura Copeland Gallery just put out an open call for Downtown Miami Art Week 2026. Artists from Miami and abroad welcome. Contact the curator Gabrielly Paz at contact@gabriellypaz.com, and check the registration link in their Instagram bio @auracopeland_gallery. Physical exhibition across their galleries on East Flagler Street, December 1-6. Google "Downtown Miami Art Week Aura Copeland" for details. Fee status unclear—reach out to confirm, but the exposure is solid.

These are the ones still open right now as of early November. Always double-check the forms because deadlines move, but these feel real and welcoming. You've got this—your work deserves to be seen.
